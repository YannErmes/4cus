Two-way Google Calendar sync

Most planners ask you to abandon your calendar. 4cus does the opposite: every day you schedule becomes an event, and anything you change in either place shows up in the other. Your calendar stays the source of truth for appointments; 4cus adds the part a calendar cannot hold — the duration, the status and the note.

  • Days you plan become events — with the right time, without typing it twice.
  • Both directions — reschedule in Google, and the grid follows.
  • Automatic — change a status and the event is updated within seconds.
  • Unlocked with a VIP key — one line in your settings, nothing else changes.

What it's for

One tool, four very different jobs

The table stays the same. What changes is the columns you add — and that is exactly why one app can cover your habits, your calendar, your task list and your time.

Habit tracking

Consistency you can actually see

Most habit apps punish you with a broken streak the moment you miss a day. 4cus shows you the month as a grid of small dots: what you did, what you skipped, what you simply never planned. A rest day is not a failure.

  • The whole month at a glance — density and completion per day, no spreadsheet maths.
  • Statuses you choose — done, in progress, skipped, or anything else you invent.
  • Durations on habits — 45 minutes of training vs 10 minutes of stretching are different things.

What you get: a truthful picture of your consistency, without the guilt spiral.

Meetings & schedule

A week that looks like your week

Meetings are just rows with a time. Set "client call — Thursdays 4pm" once and it sits in every Thursday cell until you change it. The grid becomes a real schedule, not a list with due dates.

  • A schedule column with times — click, pick, done.
  • Recurring slots without duplicating rows — one routine, many days.
  • Google Calendar sync — planned days become events, automatically, both ways.

What you get: one place that answers "when am I busy?" and "what is that meeting?" together.

Task management

A task list that knows about days

A to-do list tells you what is outstanding. It does not tell you that three of those things were planned for the same afternoon. In 4cus a task only exists on the days you put it, so the list and the plan are the same object.

  • Statuses that drive everything — nothing is "planned" while it sits idle.
  • Notes on any day — write what happened while you are still in that day.
  • Search and filters — including "only the days that have a note", which is how you find your evidence.

What you get: fewer stale tasks, because a task has to be scheduled to exist.

Time & progress

Find out what your week was really made of

This is the part other apps leave you guessing. 4cus compares the time you planned with the time you spent, per day, per task, over the last eight weeks — and shows what is still sitting undone.

  • Planned vs done vs left, to the minute.
  • Weekly progress bars — the trend, not just today.
  • Time per task — discover the project that quietly ate your October.

What you get: evidence, so next week's plan is based on facts rather than optimism.

Questions

What does the VIP key do?
It unlocks Google Calendar sync. Everything else — the table, the views, the timers, the analytics and the reminders — works exactly the same whether you use it or not.
Do I need an account or a password?
No password. You type an email and your workspace exists immediately. Use the same address on every device to get the same data everywhere.
What happens to a day I miss?
Nothing dramatic. The day stays as you left it — planned, skipped or cancelled — and you can decide later whether to move it. There is no streak to break.
Can my partner or my team use it?
Not yet: 4cus is currently a single-person workspace. Sharing and collaboration are on the list, not in the build.
Can I get it on my iPhone?
The iOS build is unsigned, so iOS will not install it on a real device without an Apple Developer signature ($99/year). On a phone today, use the web app or the Android build.
Can I move my data in from another app?
Import is not built yet. The fastest route is to recreate your routines as rows with a time — once the columns are there, the table does the rest.
